Veeam has been named a finalist in the 2026 CRN Best of the Channel Awards, recognized in the Channel Marketing Team category. It's one of the channel's most competitive honors, and a direct nod to the team behind Veeam's partner-first strategy. CRN, a brand of The Channel Company, spotlights the vendors, distributors, and solution providers setting the standard for channel excellence.
