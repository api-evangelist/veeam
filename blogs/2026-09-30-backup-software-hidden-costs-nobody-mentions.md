Backup software hidden costs are the licensing, storage, support, and recovery expenses that don’t appear in the initial quote but grow as your hardware, data, and contracts change. For a small business with a lean IT team, or no dedicated IT team at all, hidden costs tend to surface at the worst moment: a renewal invoice that jumped, a storage bill that doubled, or a restore that came with a fee nobody budgeted for.
